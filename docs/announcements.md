## Long (Reddit) — English

**Title:** Mutant Dices: dice faces, 3D dice, colorblind modes and a clean roll card for Mutant: Year Zero

Were you finding it bad that you couldn't see the numbers on your dice? Or that, when making specific rolls, the dice came out all buggy, with no card and nothing else?

That's why I made **Mutant Dices**. The things I most want you to know:

1. **The dice now have faces.**
2. **The 3D dice have them too**, with Dice So Nice.
3. **The roll card is neatly organized**, whether the roll comes from inside the sheet or from outside it.
4. **Colorblind modes.**

**About the colorblind modes:** they're there so everyone can see the dice well, with no doubt at all. Over the years I had two colorblind players at my table: one had trouble with greens, the other, I think, with red. I want the module to be accessible to everyone, and if I'm going to do something, it has to be done well: if I was going to make it for one type of color blindness, I'd make it for several, at least the most common ones. The modes are Universal (Deuteranopia / Protanopia), Tritanopia and Achromatopsia.

**Language option:** the module is in Portuguese, because it's my native language and I want Portuguese speakers to have it in their own language, and in English, so people all over the world can use it.

**Gear in the dice roller:** to change one of the module's options, you used to have to open the settings, look for Mutant Dices in the list or type its name, and only then get there. Now there's a gear in the dice roller: click it and you're in the module's options.

At my table we're currently playing Mutant: Year Zero Ad Astra, but we've already played the whole Mutant: Year Zero campaign, start to finish, and some of my players had played it back in the day too.

Basically, I made a module to make my life easier and decided to make it available online to make life easier for anyone who wants it too. If anyone has a feature that makes sense for the module, or wants something added, just say so.

- Foundry page: https://foundryvtt.com/packages/mutant-dices
- GitHub (bugs and suggestions): https://github.com/f-fabner/mutant-dices

**Título:** Mutant Dices: faces nos dados, dados 3D, modos para daltônicos e card organizado para Mutante: Ano Zero

Estava achando ruim não visualizar os números nos dados? Ou, quando fazia rolagens específicas, os dados virem todos bugados, sem card, sem nada?

Foi por isso que eu fiz o **Mutant Dices**. As coisas que eu mais quero que vocês saibam:

Quero que o módulo seja acessível para todo mundo, e se eu fiz alguma coisa, tem que ser bem feito: já que ia fazer para um tipo de daltônico, fiz para vários, pelo menos os mais comuns. **Opção de idioma:** o módulo está em português, porque é a minha língua nativa e quero que quem fala português tenha acesso no próprio idioma, e em inglês, para que gente do mundo inteiro possa usar.
