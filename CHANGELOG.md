# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10
First stable release.
- README: new screenshots of chat commands and pushing, and a disclaimer: unofficial, free, not affiliated with Free League Publishing.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-10
- Fixed `/r` rolls of Mutant dice not getting the Push button on Foundry v14.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-10
- Chat commands with only Mutant dice (`/r 3db + 2ds + 1dg`) now make the system's roll card, so they can be pushed. Text after `#` becomes the roll name.
- New world setting "Push for /r rolls" to turn this off.
- Rolls from the dice roller are made as the selected token's character.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-10
- Fixed the + and − buttons in the dice roller.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10
- New dice faces for Dice So Nice.
- Mutant: Year Zero dice shown with the new faces in chat, with a results summary for plain rolls.
- Dice roller dialog, opened from the Token controls, the chat controls or a macro.
